NYC's 311 system receives millions of service requests every year, and a significant number of them are resolved late. This project investigates where and why these delays happen — specifically whether delays are caused by location (some boroughs are inherently slower), by the type of complaint or agency handling it (some issues just take longer to fix), or by the channel used to file the complaint (phone vs. online vs. mobile). City operations and resource-planning teams could use this analysis to decide where to reallocate staff and resources, which agencies or complaint types need process improvements, and whether certain channels are creating bottlenecks. The goal is to turn raw complaint data into specific, actionable recommendations for improving response times — not just to report that delays exist, but to explain what's driving them.
